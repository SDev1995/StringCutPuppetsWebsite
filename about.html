WHO WE ARE

STRING CUT PUPPETS

String Cut Puppets are an alternative rock band from Northern Ireland, blending grunge, punk and hard rock influences into a loud and energetic live sound.

Formed in 2020, the band have spent the last few years playing venues across Ireland, releasing original music and generally making far more noise than necessary.
